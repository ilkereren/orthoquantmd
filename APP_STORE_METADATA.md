**Description:**
*Maximum 4000 characters.*

OrthoQuant MD is a professional clinical measurement tool designed for orthopedic surgeons, physical therapists, radiologists, and medical professionals. Designed for precision and speed, it allows you to perform complex measurements directly on clinical images.

**Key Features:**
- **Powerful Measuring Tools:** Measure angles, distances, circular radii, and polygon areas with specialized interactive tools.
- **Calibration Mode:** Set a reference scale (mm) to ensure 100% accurate distance and radius measurements on any image.
- **Smart Zoom 2.0:** Place measurement points with millimeter precision using 2.2x magnifier support.
- **Template Library:** Ready-to-use orthopedic templates optimized for foot, ankle, knee, hip, spine, shoulder, hand, and elbow (HVA, Q-Angle, Cobb angle, etc.).
- **Professional Organization:** Organize your measurements into patient-based folders and track clinical history (PRO).
- **Flexible Import:** HEIC support and Drag & Drop ease for iPad/macOS.
- **PDF Reporting:** Create professional PDF reports containing measurement results and clinical notes.
- **Privacy First:** All data is stored locally on your device; you have full control over your clinical data.

Streamline your clinical workflow and maximize your measurement accuracy with OrthoQuant MD.
