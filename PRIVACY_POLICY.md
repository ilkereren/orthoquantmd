# Privacy Policy for OrthoQuant MD

**Last Updated:** December 26, 2025  

OrthoQuant MD ("Application", "we", "us", "our") is a specialized clinical tool designed for orthopedic measurement and analysis. We prioritize the privacy of medical professionals and their patients by adopting a "Local-First" data architecture.

---

## 1. Medical Disclaimer and Terms of Use
**Important:** OrthoQuant MD is designed as an assistive tool specifically for **licensed medical physicians**.
- **Not a Substitute:** This Application is never a substitute for formal medical evaluation, clinical judgment, or definitive treatment decisions.
- **Physician Use Only:** Use requires advanced medical knowledge. It is not intended for use by patients or non-physicians.
- **Accuracy:** Measurement results may vary based on image quality, capture angle, and user marking precision. Physicians must consider the possibility of technical error in all measurements.
- **Responsibility:** All clinical decisions based on the Application's output are the sole responsibility of the attending physician.

## 2. Introduction and Data Sovereignty
Unlike many modern applications, OrthoQuant MD does not utilize a centralized database for your clinical records. **You own your data.** All patient identifiers, radiographic images, measurement results, and clinical notes are stored exclusively in the Application's private storage on your physical device (iPhone, iPad, or Mac).

## 3. Clinical Data Privacy (Zero-Cloud Policy)
- **No Remote Storage:** We do not upload clinical data to any servers. There is no "cloud" where your patient data resides.
- **No Third-Party Access:** Because the data never leaves your device, neither we nor any third parties can access, view, or process your clinical measurements.
- **Responsibility:** Data security is managed by the device's operating system (iOS/macOS). We recommend using biometric locks (FaceID/TouchID) and maintaining encrypted backups via your personal iCloud or local machine.

## 4. Subscription Management and Purchasing Data
To provide **OrthoQuant MD PRO** features, we utilize **RevenueCat** to manage subscription states and processing. 

### 3.1 Data Handled by RevenueCat
To manage your premium access, the following metadata is processed:
- **Transaction Information:** Purchase dates, renewal dates, and anonymous transaction IDs.
- **Entitlement Status:** Whether your account has active PRO access.
- **System Information:** App version and device OS version (to ensure compatibility).

*Crucially, RevenueCat does not have access to any clinical measurements or patient images.* You can review RevenueCat’s privacy practices at [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

### 3.2 Payment Processing
All financial transactions are handled securely by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store**. OrthoQuant MD does not collect or store credit card numbers or sensitive financial data.

## 5. Analytical Data and Diagnostics
We may collect anonymous diagnostic and usage metadata (e.g., app crashes or which tools are most frequently used) to improve the Application's performance. This data is strictly non-identifiable and cannot be linked back to individual patients or clinical cases.

## 6. Security of Local Data
The Application utilizes secure, sandboxed storage provided by the operating system. If you delete the Application, all locally stored clinical data will be permanently removed unless you have performed a manual backup or export of reports.

## 7. User Rights & Compliance (GDPR)
As a clinical user, you are the **Data Controller** for the patient information you manage within OrthoQuant MD. 
- **Right to Access/Delete:** Since data is local, you have full control to edit or delete any record at any time.
- **Data Portability:** You can export measurements as PDF or Image files to integrate them into your Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy to reflect technical or legal changes. Significant updates will be communicated through the Application’s "Release Notes" section in Settings.

## 9. Contact Information
For questions regarding this policy or technical support, please reach out via our official communication channels.
